# NJ237_Project Engineer_New Jersey

KS Engineers, P.C. is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.

Position: Project Engineer

Reports to: Project Manager

General Qualities

Strong written and oral communication skills to coordinate the activities

Proactive self-starter capable of setting a positive example for the employees

Ability to supervise engineering staff and provide technical input/review of their work

Duties/Responsibilities

Expected to work well in team environment and oversee or assist in the oversight of the project team including all architects, designers, engineers, contractors, vendors, and consultants

Exhibit real time understanding of project status (all facets) and be able to develop, distribute and discuss projects updates as required

Responsible for the vetting and hiring of project consultants and vendors to ensure value and achievement of customer goals

Ensure that all local, state, and national building codes and regulations and safety precautions are followed.

Optimization in terms of project schedule, resources, supply chain, cost & quality

Obtains all necessary regulations and permits.

Develops and adheres to the budget, timeline, and quality control plan

Perform constructability reviews, pricing, and cost impacts due to phasing and timing

Experience and Educational Requirements

Preferred 5-10 years of experience in structural engineering (Buildings or bridges)

Bachelor’s degree in civil engineering.

Licensed Professional Engineer preferably in NY and NJ.

Possesses familiarity with Microsoft Office (Project, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, etc.), Bluebeam, and Project scheduling software.
